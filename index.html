Quotebloom privacy policy

Quotebloom: Quote Builder, by Techie Bravo. Effective 17 November 2026.

Summary

Quotebloom does not collect, send, or share any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device, and the app works fully offline.

What the app stores

Quotes you save to the library (text, author, date, tags, styling and a small preview image), photos you choose as backgrounds, and your settings. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Links

The store and email links on the About page open in other apps (Google Play, your email app). Quotebloom itself sends nothing.

Photos

When you choose a photo, the app keeps a copy in its private storage so your saved quotes still show it. Photos are never uploaded. You are responsible for having the rights to any photo you use.

Sharing and saving

When you share an image, it goes only to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. When you save, it goes to your gallery or the folder you choose.

Backups

App data is excluded from Android cloud backup, so it is not copied to any server.

Deleting your data

Delete quotes in the Library, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be posted here.
